# Uyuyan Ejderha — Art Bible

Reference: `public/ui/keyart.webp` (title screen). Every asset must look like it was cut out of that picture.

## Style
- Stylized cartoon 3D, chunky rounded proportions, hand-painted textures with soft gradients and a few painted highlights. Think toy figurine, not realism, not low-poly.
- Silhouettes readable from a high top-down camera: big heads, big hands and feet, bold shapes.
- No black outlines on models; the HUD carries the outlined look.

## Palette
| Role | Colours |
|---|---|
| Cave stone | violet-plum `#3b2c4d` → lavender highlights `#8a76a8` |
| Treasure | warm gold `#ffc23a`, amber shadows `#c97a12` |
| Magic | crystal teal `#5ee7ff`, violet `#b46bff`, pink `#ff7ad9` |
| Fire | `#fff1a0` → `#ff7a1a` → `#6b1a10` |
| Rarity (eggs, babies) | common `#9be07a`, rare `#46a8ff`, epic `#b655ff`, legendary `#ffcf3a`, divine `#f6f2ff` |

## Light rules
- Warm gold light from the hoard in the centre, cool violet ambient at the edges.
- Glow belongs to things that are magical: crystals, eggs, baby dragons, fire, the dragon's eyes. Each glows in **its own colour** (egg = its rarity colour, baby = its rarity colour).
- No flat neon rings on the floor. Ownership and selection are shown with painted, in-world things: carved stone rims, coloured banners, a soft shadow blob plus a small painted marker.

## Cast
- **Pati (default thief):** kid in an oversized hoodie with the hood up, black bandit eye-mask, sneaky tiptoe stance, small loot sack. The hoodie is light grey so the wardrobe can tint it.
- **Tilki (unlock):** fox kid bandit, red scarf, mask, bushy tail.
- **Rakun (unlock, replaces Jessie):** raccoon kid bandit; the raccoon's own markings are the mask. Striped tail, little vest.
- **Ejderha:** big chubby red dragon, gold horns and belly plates, small wings folded, cute-but-imposing face, sleeps curled on the hoard.
- **Yavru ejderha:** chibi baby dragon, huge eyes, stubby wings, pale cream body so rarity colour can tint and glow it.
- **Yumurta:** dragon egg with scale pattern and a crack line, pale shell for rarity tint, glows from inside.

## Props
Woven twig nest with straw and feathers; banner pole with a cloth pennant (white cloth, tinted per owner); crystal-topped rock spires; stone brazier with iron bowl; gold coin piles in three sizes with gems and a goblet; crystal clusters; glowing mushrooms; stalagmites.
